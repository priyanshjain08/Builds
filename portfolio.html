how a project runs

Five steps, start to launch.

The same order every time, so you always know what's happening and what's next.

01

Discovery call

We talk through your goals, audience and what "done" actually looks like before anything gets designed.

~30 min
02

Wireframe & content

I map out every page and section in low-fidelity, so structure gets agreed on before visuals do.

2–3 days
03

Visual design

Full comps in Figma, using the eye trained from years in graphic design — typography, colour and spacing, considered.

3–5 days
04

Development

Hand-coded build, responsive from the first line, tested across real browsers and devices — not just resized.

1–2 weeks
05

Launch & handover

Deployed, documented, and handed over with a short walkthrough so you're never locked out of your own site.

1 day
