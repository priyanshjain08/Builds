Featured project — personal work

01
DevDesk

The problem

Developers juggle several small tools through the day — timers, snippets, task lists — and lose momentum switching between them. Nothing kept the essentials in one place without getting in the way.

The idea

A single desktop dashboard that surfaces what's relevant right now: today's tasks, a focus timer, and quick access to saved code snippets — built to stay out of the way until it's needed.

How it works
